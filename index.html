Decision 1 · Buy first, it's 5 days out

Seats

Resale only now. The cheapest seat is $236 and the average ticket is $619. Above about $600, paying more doesn't always mean a better night. Club seats cost about the same as regular ones but come with real perks.

What club seats get you

  • Private entrance: Entry E (southeast corner), the closest gate to the hotels.
  • Climate-controlled lounge and private restrooms. No concourse lines.
  • Wider padded armchairs and extra legroom.
  • Side view of both the main stage and the catwalk.

Founders Club (C210) is all-inclusive for Cowboys games only. At concerts you pay for food and drinks.

Skip these

  • Floor GA, $713–875. No seats. You'd line up 2+ hours before doors and stand for 5+ hours. It costs more than club seats.
  • Standing room, $262–553. A crowded end-zone deck with no seats.
  • Section 458. The stage is partly blocked from there.
Where to buy. Compare a few sites; the same section can cost very different amounts. SeatGeek ($243 floor) and Event Escapes ($276) usually beat Ticketmaster resale ($324 floor), but Event Escapes only delivers tickets 24–48 hours before the show. Ticketmaster, StubHub and AXS send verified tickets right away. With the show 5 days out, instant delivery is the safer bet.
Decision 2 · Saturday night

Hotel

The worst part of AT&T Stadium is getting out. There's no train, and Ubers surge after the show. Stay within walking distance and you can skip the car entirely. The two Loews hotels below are the only luxury options close enough. A covered skybridge connects them, so guests at either can use both hotels' restaurants, bars and spa.

Live! by Loews

  • 5–10 min walk on a paved path to the stadium.
  • Next door to Texas Live! (Lockhart Smokehouse, Sports & Social).
  • Cut & Bourbon steakhouse, Arlington Clover Club cocktails.
  • Suites: Live! 616–663 sq ft · Premium Stadium 767–792 · Grand Stadium 936.
  • The rooftop infinity pool is seasonal and may be closed.

Loews Arlington

  • 10–15 min walk. Newest resort in the district, 888 rooms.
  • Soy Cowboy (sushi, robata, Korean BBQ) and Farena (Italian, open until 11 PM Sat).
  • The Bar serves food until midnight; open until 1:30 AM. Room service 24 hrs.
  • Full spa on floor 2. Year-round pool and hot tub.
  • Rooms from ~$229. Stadium-view rooms 718–773 sq ft.

Valet at either hotel costs $39/night, and you can come and go. Park once and walk for the rest of the weekend.

Decision 3 · Optional

Afterparty

The official afterparty is hosted by Justin LaBoy at Vice Park, 2601 Gaston Ave, Dallas. It's 21+ and doors open at 10 PM. Shazam lists it as a Ye concert, but it's a nightlife event. Ye's set is still running when doors open, and the party is about 20 miles east.
