Step 1 of 3 · Do this first

Seats

How do you want to watch the show?

The show is 5 days away, so tickets are resale only. Prices go from $236 to over $1,000 a seat. Think about what matters to you: being close, being comfortable, skipping lines, or spending less.

What "club" seats mean

  • Your own entrance (Entry E), which is the closest gate to the hotels.
  • An indoor lounge and private bathrooms.
  • Bigger, padded seats.
  • Food and drinks are not free at concerts.

The report says to skip these

  • Floor ($713–875). No seats. You stand for 5+ hours and must line up early for a good spot.
  • Standing room ($262–553). No seat, and it's crowded.
  • Section 458. The stage is partly blocked.
Where to buy Check two or three sites, because prices differ. SeatGeek is often cheapest. Ticketmaster and StubHub send your tickets right away. Some cheaper sites only send them 1–2 days before the show.
Step 2 of 3

Hotel

What do you want from the hotel?

Getting out after the show is the hard part. There's no train, and traffic and Uber prices are bad. Both hotels below are close enough to walk. They're joined by an indoor bridge, so you can use the restaurants and spa at both.

Live! by Loews

  • 5–10 minute walk to the stadium.
  • Right next to Texas Live! (bars and restaurants).
  • Steakhouse (Cut & Bourbon) and a cocktail bar.
  • Rooftop pool may be closed for the season.

Loews Arlington

  • 10–15 minute walk to the stadium.
  • Sushi (Soy Cowboy) and Italian (Farena, open until 11 PM).
  • Bar serves food until midnight. Room service all night.
  • Full spa. Pool and hot tub open all year.

Parking at either hotel is $39 a night with valet. Park once and walk the rest of the weekend.

Step 3 of 3 · Optional

Afterparty

Do you want to keep going after the show?

The official afterparty is at Vice Park in Dallas, hosted by Justin LaBoy. It's not a second Ye concert. Doors open at 10 PM, but the show is still going then, and the party is about 20 miles away. You must be 21+.
